Privacy

Frames are saved on this phone only. Faces are blurred with a basic detector that misses side-on, small and helmeted faces. Number plates are not blurred yet. Keep recordings on your phone or your own computer, and blur faces and plates fully before anyone else sees them.

Developer

Simulations send made-up hazards at a made-up 10 m/s, without position. Desk tests only: never ride with one on. Takes effect the next time the camera starts.
